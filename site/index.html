Every day takes care of itself

Fill the queue once in a while. Everything else happens on its own, and you only hear about it when something needs you.

  1. A new question goes up

    At the time you choose, the next approved question becomes the question of the day. When the queue runs out, the question asked longest ago comes back, so there's always one.

  2. Members answer

    Logged-in members answer, reply to each other, like answers and vote for good questions. Joining offers them the daily email.

  3. Claude checks each answer

    Clean answers appear at once. Doubtful ones wait for you, and swearing, spam and harassment never appear. Members can report anything that slips through.

  4. Email and social posts go out

    The new question is emailed to your list with yesterday's top answers, and posted through Zapier, Make or n8n with its share image.

  5. The queue tops itself up

    When it gets short, Claude writes a batch of new questions for your approval. Or you write them all yourself.

What's in it

  • A queue you control

    Add questions one per line, drag to reorder, approve or reject, and see the day each will be asked.

  • Questions written by Claude

    On demand or automatically, with guidance you edit. A big variety by default, and politics only rarely.

  • Answers, votes and replies

    Built on WordPress comments, so Akismet, the Comments screen and your moderation tools all work.

  • Automatic moderation

    Every answer is checked against your guidelines. Nothing appears unchecked, even if the check can't run.

  • Your mailing list

    Members join FluentCRM on this site or another, with double opt-in and a connection test.

  • The daily email

    Sent as a FluentCRM campaign, or by a simple built-in sender with unsubscribe links.

  • Share images and links

    A 1200×630 image for every question, share tags, share links and signed webhook posts.

  • Fits your site

    Your logo and colours, Automatic.css, blocks, shortcodes, and query arguments for Etch and other builders.

  • Works with To-do Hub

    A task when the queue runs low, answers wait for review, or the email or a post fails.
