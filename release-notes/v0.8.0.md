# Pulse Question 0.8.0

More ways in, and more reasons to answer.

- **Sign in with Google.** Members can join or log in with their Google account instead of making a password. *Continue with Google* appears on the log-in and sign-up pages and in the answer box. Set it up under **Settings → Members → Sign in with Google**: create a *Web application* client in Google Cloud, add the redirect address shown there, and paste in the client ID and secret (or set `PULSE_QUESTION_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `PULSE_QUESTION_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` in `wp-config.php`).
  - A Google account is linked to the member with the same verified email address. Otherwise a new member is made, when *Anyone can register* is on.
  - Editors and administrators keep signing in with their password.
  - New members get the mailing-list boxes with their first answer.
- **Answer from the home page.** Today's question has its own answer box (or Google, log-in and join buttons for guests). After posting, the member stays on the page and sees a thank-you.
- **Yesterday's top answers** show under today's question until today has some.
- **Recent answers:** the five newest answers to any question, each with the question it answers.
- **Past question cards** have an answers button (*12 answers*, or *Answer*), a light wash of the site's second colour and a stripe of the main colour. Set `--pq-page-bg` if your page isn't white.
- **Settings → Answers** has the number of recent answers and a switch for the answer box. The block and `[pulse_question]` can override them (`recent="0"`, `box="0"`).
- **For page builders such as Etch:** `[pulse_question_answer_box]`, `[pulse_question_top_answers]`, `[pulse_question_recent_answers]`, and `[pulse_question_answers_button]` for a past-questions loop.
