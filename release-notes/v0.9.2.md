# Pulse Question 0.9.2

Your own sender name on the daily email.

- **FluentCRM sender.** With FluentCRM, the daily email used FluentCRM's default sender, which is usually your business rather than your question site. Under **Settings → Daily email → FluentCRM sender**, set the name (for example "Pulse Question"), the address it comes from and, optionally, a reply-to. FluentCRM needs the name and address together. Only the daily email changes; your other FluentCRM campaigns keep their sender.
