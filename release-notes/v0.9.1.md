# Pulse Question 0.9.1

Neater share links.

- **Icons instead of words.** Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky, email and copy link are now round icons in each network's own colour (email in your second colour, copy link in your main colour).
- **Hover labels.** Hovering over an icon, or tabbing to it, shows its name in a small label in your main colour. Screen readers hear "Share on Facebook (opens in a new tab)" and so on. Change the label colour with `--pq-tip-bg`.
- **"Share this question"** is the new heading for the icons.
- **Before the answers.** On today's question, the share icons now come before the answer box.
- **`[pulse_question_share]`** shows a question's share icons anywhere, such as in an Etch layout (today's question, or `id="12"` for another).
