# Pulse Question 0.5.0

The daily email and social sharing.

## Daily email
- **Each new question, emailed:** sent about a minute after it's asked, once per question day. It has the question, its background, an *Answer today's question* button, and the three most-liked answers to yesterday's question.
- **With FluentCRM** (on this site or another): sent as a FluentCRM campaign to your daily email lists, to subscribed contacts only. FluentCRM adds its footer and unsubscribe link, sends through your email service and reports opens and clicks. If a try fails partway, the next try finishes the same draft instead of making another.
- **Without FluentCRM:** a built-in sender emails members who chose the daily email, 40 a minute, with an unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe for mail apps.
- **Settings:** subject (`{question}`, `{date}`, `{site}`), an opening line, the button text, the number of answers, and the buttons *Preview*, *Send me a test* and *Send today's email now*. Turning it on starts with the next question, so nobody gets a surprise email mid-day.
- **If it fails,** it's tried again hourly (up to five times), and To-do Hub gets a task until it works.

## Social sharing
- **Share image** for every question (1200×630): your logo, the label, the question and your site's address. A featured image on a question is used instead.
- **Share tags** on question pages, so shared links show the question and image. With Yoast SEO or Rank Math, the plugin hands them the image instead of writing its own tags.
- **Share links** under each question: Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky, email, copy link, and the phone's share sheet. These are plain links, with no third-party scripts.
- **Automatic posts:** add a webhook from Zapier, Make, n8n, Pabbly or IFTTT. Each new question is sent with its link, a ready-written post (your template and hashtags) and the share image, and your automation posts it wherever you connect. Requests are signed. There's a test button, and failures are retried and get a To-do task.

## Fixes
- With FluentCRM on another site, double opt-in contacts now get FluentCRM's confirmation email. Before, FluentCRM's REST API only sends it when asked, so they stayed pending.
- The Members settings and *Test connection* name the FluentCRM permissions the user needs:
  - *Add, update and import contacts*;
  - *Create and update tags, lists, companies and segments*;
  - *Write and send emails*, for the daily email.
