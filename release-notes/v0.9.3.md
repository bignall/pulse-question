# Pulse Question 0.9.3

A proper home screen icon.

- **Home screen icon.** WordPress uses your site icon for iPhones and iPads too, and they fill its see-through corners with black. A site icon with rounded corners or a transparent background therefore gets black corners on the home screen. Under **Settings → Branding → Home screen icon**, choose a square image with a solid background instead; the phone rounds the corners itself. Browser tabs keep using the site icon.
