# Pulse Question 0.9.6

Password managers keep their hands off your settings.

- **LastPass and other password managers can't change these settings any more.** 0.9.5 stopped Chrome's own autofill, but extensions such as LastPass fill fields with their own script and kept putting your login email in the Google client ID. The Google client ID and secret, the mailing list user name and application password, and the Anthropic API key now only change when you type, paste or drop text into them. Anything else is put back straight away and again just before saving, so nothing filled in by a password manager is saved.
