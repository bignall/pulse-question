# Pulse Question 0.9.4

A tidier Settings page.

- **Tabs.** Settings is split into General, Claude, Answers, Members, Mailing list, Daily email, Social and Branding, so you see one section at a time. Links from the setup checklist and To-do Hub open the right tab.
- **One Save button for everything.** It stays at the bottom of the frame and saves every tab, then brings you back to the tab you were on. Tabs with unsaved changes get a dot, the save bar lists them, you're warned before leaving the page with unsaved changes, and Cmd/Ctrl+S saves.
- **Members and Mailing list are separate.** Sign-up and Google sign-in are under **Members**. The sign-up box labels, the FluentCRM connection and **Test connection** are under **Mailing list**.
- **Fixed:** the colour pickers under **Branding** were hiding their labels.
