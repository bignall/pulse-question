# Pulse Question 0.3.0

Questions written by Claude.

- **Write questions** (Questions → Queue): choose how many and, if you like, a theme. Claude writes them with a short background line and a category, and leaves out repeats of questions you already have.
- **Automatic top-up** (Settings): when fewer than 14 days of questions are in the queue or waiting for approval, a batch of 10 is written on the hourly check. At most once every 6 hours, backing off after failures. Both numbers are yours to change.
- **Approval:** Claude's questions wait for your approval, marked *Claude* in the Queue, unless you turn on auto-approve.
- **Guidance** you can edit. The default asks for big variety and keeps politics to rare occasions.
- **Settings:** Anthropic API key (or `PULSE_QUESTION_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in `wp-config.php`), model (Claude Opus 5.5 by default) and effort, plus a log of recent batches with token counts.

Uses the official Anthropic PHP SDK, bundled and prefixed so it can't clash with other plugins, and sending requests through WordPress's own HTTP API. The download is larger (about 6 MB) because of the SDK.
