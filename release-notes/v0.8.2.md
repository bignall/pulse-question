# Pulse Question 0.8.2

Fixes special characters showing as codes. WordPress turns a question's straight quotes into curly ones, stored as codes like `&#8217;`, and these weren't turned back into characters everywhere. A question such as *If you had to eat one country's food…* showed as `country&#8217;s` in:

- automatic social posts (the webhook's `question`, `text` and `image_alt`);
- the daily email's subject, heading and yesterday's question;
- the text of the X, Bluesky and email share links;
- share tags (`og:title`, the site name).

Nothing else changes.
