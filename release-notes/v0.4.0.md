# Pulse Question 0.4.0

Answers from members, checked automatically.

- **Members answer:** logged-in members answer the question of the day, reply to each other (one level deep), mark a question as *Good question* and like answers. Answers sort by *Top* or *Newest*. Guests can read answers and see buttons to log in or join.
- **Mailing list:** every new member is offered the daily question email and news and offers. The boxes are on the sign-up form, or appear with a member's first answer. Both are ticked to begin with and neither is required. A setting turns off the pre-ticking; in the EU and UK a pre-ticked box doesn't count as consent. Sign-ups go to FluentCRM on this site or on another one, with double opt-in, list and tag ids, retries every hour if a sign-up fails, and a log in Settings.
- **Automatic moderation:** Claude checks each answer against guidelines you can edit. Fine answers appear straight away; doubtful ones (links, self-harm, campaigning, off-topic) wait under Comments → Pending; swearing, slurs, harassment, sexual content, spam and personal details are kept off the site. If the check can't run, the answer is held and checked again later, so nothing appears unchecked. Other modes: hold everything, or WordPress's own settings.
- **Reports:** answers reported by three members (you can change the number) are hidden until you look.
- **Admin:** a *Check* column under Comments with each verdict and reason, plus a To-do Hub task when answers are waiting.

**To set up:** turn on Settings → General → *Anyone can register* (role: Subscriber), add your Anthropic API key, and choose your FluentCRM lists under Questions → Settings → Members.
