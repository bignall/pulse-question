# Pulse Question 0.9.0

Ready for Instagram.

- **A portrait image for Instagram.** Each question now also gets a 1080×1350 JPEG, Instagram's tallest feed shape, in the same design as the share image with a larger question. Instagram only accepts JPEG, so the existing 1200×630 PNG can't be used there.
- **An Instagram caption.** Links in Instagram captions can't be clicked, so the caption says *Share your answer at example.com (link in bio)* instead of including the address, followed by your hashtags.
- **Two new webhook fields:** `instagram_image_url` and `instagram_text`. In Make, use them in an **Instagram for Business → Create a Photo Post** step. The Instagram account must be a professional account linked to your Facebook Page.

Nothing else changes; Facebook and other networks keep using `image_url` and `text`.
