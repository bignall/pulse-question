# Pulse Question 0.6.0

Ready for other sites.

- **Branding** (Settings → Branding):
  - Choose the logo for share images: your site's logo or icon, the Pulse Question logo, or any image from the Media Library.
  - Choose a main colour and a second colour. They're used on the site, in the daily email and on share images. Leave them empty to follow Automatic.css.
  - Sites upgrading from 0.5 keep the Pulse Question logo until they choose another.
- **From name and address** for the built-in daily email.
- **Setup checklist** at the top of Settings, and a notice after activation that leads to it. It covers questions, showing the question, the Claude key, registration, the mailing list, the daily email, branding and social posts. Steps the plugin can't see can be marked as done.
- **Guides** at https://bignall.github.io/pulse-question/, covering:
  - getting started, questions, answers and moderation;
  - members and the mailing list, including FluentCRM permissions and what each Test connection result means;
  - the daily email, social sharing, branding and design;
  - developers, and privacy and data.
- **Tested with WordPress 7.1.** All test suites pass on 7.1.5, and the code now passes the WordPress coding standards.
- **Uninstalling** with "delete all data" on also removes members' mailing-list choices. Their accounts stay.
